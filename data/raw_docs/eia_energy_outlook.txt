U.S. Energy Information Administration - Annual Energy Outlook
Sample excerpt - Phase 1 test fixture, not an authoritative copy.

Petroleum and Natural Gas Production
Crude oil production is projected to remain near record levels through the projection
period as producers develop tight oil resources. Natural gas production grows in
response to demand from the liquefied natural gas export sector and from domestic
electric power generation.

Refining and Product Supply
Refinery utilization rates remain sensitive to crude oil price differentials and to
regional demand for distillate fuel oil. Petroleum product exports continue to grow,
supported by refining capacity in the Gulf Coast region.

Electric Power Sector
Renewable generating capacity, principally solar photovoltaic and wind, accounts for
the majority of capacity additions. Natural gas fired combined cycle plants continue
to provide dispatchable generation to balance intermittent renewable output. Retirement
of coal fired capacity continues across the projection period.

Energy Consumption by Sector
Industrial energy consumption grows fastest among end use sectors, driven by bulk
chemicals and refining. Transportation energy consumption declines as vehicle fuel
economy improves and electric vehicle adoption increases. Buildings sector consumption
remains relatively flat as efficiency gains offset floorspace growth.
