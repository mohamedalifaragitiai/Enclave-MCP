# NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)

Sample excerpt - Phase 1 test fixture, not an authoritative copy.

## Govern

The Govern function cultivates a culture of risk management within organizations
designing, developing, deploying, evaluating, or acquiring AI systems. Policies,
processes, procedures, and practices across the organization related to the mapping,
measuring, and managing of AI risks are in place, transparent, and implemented
effectively.

## Map

The Map function establishes the context to frame risks related to an AI system.
Context is recognized and risks related to context are identified. Categorization of
the AI system is performed. Capabilities, targeted usage, goals, and expected benefits
and costs compared with appropriate benchmarks are understood.

## Measure

The Measure function employs quantitative, qualitative, or mixed-method tools,
techniques, and methodologies to analyze, assess, benchmark, and monitor AI risk and
related impacts. AI systems are evaluated for trustworthy characteristics including
validity and reliability, safety, security and resilience, accountability and
transparency, explainability and interpretability, privacy, and fairness.

## Manage

The Manage function entails allocating risk resources to mapped and measured risks on
a regular basis and as defined by the Govern function. Risk treatment comprises plans
to respond to, recover from, and communicate about incidents or events. Mechanisms are
in place to supersede, disengage, or deactivate AI systems that demonstrate performance
or outcomes inconsistent with intended use.
